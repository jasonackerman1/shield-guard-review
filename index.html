Commenting as

How to review this video

  1. Press play. Click the play button on the video. Use the speaker button on the video to turn the sound up or down.
  2. See something to change? Click the blue "Add comment" button under the video. The video stops on that spot for you. The first time, it asks for your name.
  3. Type your note in the box, for example "This slide moves too fast." Then click Save comment. A small picture of that moment is saved with your note.
  4. Press play again and keep watching. Add as many comments as you like. They appear in the "Your comments" list.
  5. When you're finished, click "Send my comments" and follow the two steps it shows: download your review (a PDF), then email it to Jason.

Need a break? You can close this page any time. Your comments are kept on this computer. Come back to this same link on the same computer, using the same web browser (for example Chrome or Edge), and everything will still be here. The video starts where you stopped.

Questions and problems
The video won't play.
Wait a few seconds, then click play again. If it still won't start, refresh the page: press F5 on Windows, or Cmd + R on a Mac. Your comments won't be lost.
Where did my comments go?
They're saved only on the computer and web browser you used. A different computer, a different browser, or a private/incognito window won't show them. Go back to the one you used before.
I made a mistake in a comment.
Click Delete under that comment, then add a new one.
How do I jump back to a comment?
Click the blue time (like 4:22) or the small picture on any comment. The video jumps to that moment. The orange dots under the video show where your comments are; you can click those too.
How do I find a certain slide?
Click Slides (22) under the video to see the list, then click a slide to jump to it.
Can I use my phone?
Yes, but a computer is easier. Your comments stay on the device you used.
Who sees my comments?
Only Jason, once you send them. Nobody else can see them on this page.
